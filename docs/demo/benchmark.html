The benchmark

Nemotron Super injected one kind of slop into each of 45 functions. The functions come from 15 SWE-bench Lite instances of 7 repositories. Hidden tests run all of them, and the gate never sees those tests. The whole run cost $1.16.

  • 91% flagged: 41 of 45 injected items.
  • 34% of the flagged items got a proven fix: 14 of 41.
  • 0 of the proven fixes broke a hidden test.

Some kinds get few fixes yet: redundant_check has 0 of 9, swallowed_error has 1 of 8, wrapper has 1 of 7. The gate flags them and writes a review note.
